# Kamil Szczepanik

Senior AI Engineer, product-minded, based in Dubai. I build LLM features end to end, from client brief to production, and prove they work with evals.

## What I've shipped

- **Platero AI** (founder): AI replies to restaurant Google reviews in the owner's voice. Cut hallucinations from 19% to under 2%, measured with an LLM-as-a-judge eval.
- **Safety Support** (co-founder): a RAG assistant on expert-curated safety material, with a hard-coded emergency number and a 316-case red team on the OWASP LLM Top 10.
- **CAMB.AI**: cut latency by 50% in a production real-time voice translation app, and built an AI website translator for 120 languages, live on a major European sports federation's website.
- **RaffleKing** (Plug Marketing): built most of a prize platform with 5,773 users, 884 raffles and $30,000+ paid to 402 winners.
- **Reflex** (released on iOS): a real-time multiplayer card game, built solo on React Native and WebSockets.

Before AI: Lead Frontend Engineer at SKY ENGINE AI, and 5+ years of full-stack TypeScript.
